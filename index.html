NPEGC in short

NEUILLE PRIME is a multigaming esports org from France, playing under the tag NP67. It fields a Rocket League roster and a League of Legends roster; the Rocket League side has its own coach. Players, coaches and staff sit in one structure: NEUILLE PRIME ESPORT GAMING COMPANY.
