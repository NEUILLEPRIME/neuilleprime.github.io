About NPEGC

An esports org from France, built around two games.

Where NPEGC stands

NEUILLE PRIME is a multigaming esports org based in France. It plays under the tag NP67 and runs as one structure: NEUILLE PRIME ESPORT GAMING COMPANY.

Two games, two rosters. Rocket League is played 3v3 with a coach; League of Legends fields a full five.

One tag. Two games. Built in France.

Behind the players, two staff roles keep things running: management and media. Tryouts, partnerships and content requests all go through the same inbox and Discord.

Tryouts, partners, content

How do tryouts work?

Tryouts are announced on the NPEGC Discord. Join the server and watch the announcements, or email NPEGC with your game, your role and your rank.

Can my brand partner with NPEGC?

Every request gets read. Email NPEGC with who you are and what you have in mind, and management will get back to you.

Does NPEGC stream or make content?

Content goes out on the Twitch, YouTube and social accounts linked at the bottom of this page. For a collab, email NPEGC.
